02 / Role of XRP

XRP is native.

XRP is not an issued token, and it has specific ledger-level roles in payments, transaction costs and account reserves.

Transaction
Network fee
Fee destroyed
Why is the fee destroyed?

Transaction fees deter spam and are not distributed as mining rewards. A validated transaction may consume a small amount of XRP for fees.

03 / Core design philosophies

Why XRPL works this way

Open participation

The protocol is open-source, with network participation and validation rules independent of this educational site.

Agreement over mining

Consensus provides transaction ordering and ledger validation without proof-of-work mining.

Built-in asset features

Payments, issued assets, NFTs and exchange mechanisms are ledger-level protocol concepts.
